One person, many things to explore.

This journal brings together two parts of my life that constantly influence each other: the technical pursuit of understanding how things work, and the personal pursuit of understanding what it means to experience life.

Here, engineering and research can sit alongside everyday observations, books, questions, lessons, and moments worth remembering. Some writing will be detailed and technical; some will be unfinished thoughts. Both belong here.
